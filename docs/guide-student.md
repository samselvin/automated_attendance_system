# Student user guide

## Signing in

Use your college email with the temp password from your Admin/college
office (you'll be asked to set a new one the first time), or "Sign in
with Google" if enabled for the college. There's no self-registration —
your account has to already exist before you can sign in.

## Home

Your overall attendance percentage and today's classes at a glance, plus
a bell icon (top of every screen) for notifications — leave/OD decisions,
low-attendance warnings, and anything else that needs your attention.

## Attendance

- **Overall** — your attendance percentage across every class, using the
  college's configured rule for how Leave and On-Duty count (they're
  always shown separately from plain absence, never mixed in silently).
- **Subject-wise** — the same breakdown per subject, so you can see
  exactly which subject is dragging your percentage down.
- **History** — your past Leave/OD requests and their current status.

Your percentage is only ever calculated from periods that were actually
**held** — a holiday, a cancelled period, or a period nobody has taken
attendance for yet is never counted against you either way.

## Apply for Leave / OD

From **Attendance → Apply**, submit a Leave, Medical, or On-Duty request
for a date (or date range). What happens next depends on the type:

- **Leave / Medical** — approved once either your Class Advisor **or**
  the HOD approves it.
- **On-Duty** — stays **pending** until **both** your Class Advisor and
  the HOD approve it. If either one rejects it, it's rejected immediately
  — the other approval (if it already happened) doesn't override that.

If attendance for that date was already taken before your request is
approved, approval automatically updates your attendance record — you
don't need to ask a teacher to fix it by hand.

## Academics

Once an Admin **publishes** a semester's results, you'll see:

- Your internal marks per subject (the CAT/Class Test/Assignment/MCQ
  breakdown, using your regulation's actual weighting).
- Your official semester grade per subject, your **SGPA** for that
  semester, and your running **CGPA**.

Nothing here is visible before publishing — entering marks and publishing
results are two separate steps specifically so a mark being entered never
means it's final yet.

## Events

Workshops, seminars, exams, sports days, and other events relevant to
you — whole-college, your department, your year, your class, or a
student group you're in — show up on your Home screen and at
`/student/events`, and you're notified the moment one is published.

## Timetable

A read-only weekly view of your class's schedule, including which bell
schedule (period timings) applies on which day.

## Profile

Your own details on file (roll number, register number, department,
regulation, parent contact) — for corrections, contact your college
office; students can't edit this themselves, since it's the kind of
record Section 41 requires never be silently rewritten.
